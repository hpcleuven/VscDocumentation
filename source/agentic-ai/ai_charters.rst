Institutional AI Charters
=========================

Every institution associated with each VSC site has established internal
policies regarding the use and governance of AI technologies. The aim for
such AI policies is to ensure secure, responsible, ethical yet productive
use of AI tools for the institute affiliates.

.. note::

   In any form, the use of `AI tools <ai_tools>`_ within VSC adheres to the local 
   AI policy of the corresponding institution.
