###############################
:fas:`robot` Agentic AI and HPC
###############################

We are living in the booming era of Agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI).
Given the very close connection between HPC and AI, the two fields of research
are evolving hand-in-hand, and progress in one has impact on the progress of the
other.
Hence, VSC users can make use of (agentic) AI tools on the VSC cloud and compute
facilities, provided that they adhere to the best practices set forth in this document.

Given the fast-paced advancement of AI technologies, this part of the VSC documentation
would also keep evolving to reflect upon the best practices of using AI-assisted tools
within VSC.
