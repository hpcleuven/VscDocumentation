:fas:`robot` Agentic AI and HPC
###############################

Purpose
=======
Artificial intelligence (hereafter AI) tools have increasing capabilities which can
improve research productivity, but they also introduce new risks or adverse side effects
on overall cluster operation that differ substantially from how an individual user interacts
with the cluster. The documentation in this section can be regarded as a baseline and best
practices for the use of agentic AI, autonomous workflows, coding assistants, large language
models (LLMs), and traditional machine-learning applications on the VSC HPC and cloud systems.


Scope
=====

The following sections provide best-practice guidelines which are approved by your local
VSC support team, meaning that VSC users are strongly recommended to follow them.
Applications and workflows which deviate from these guidelines are subject to termination
by the VSC support team.

Given the fast-paced advancement of AI technologies, this part of the VSC documentation
would also keep evolving to reflect upon the best practices of using AI-assisted tools
within VSC.
